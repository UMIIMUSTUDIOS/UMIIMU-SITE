WELCOME TO THE UMIIMU UNIVERSE

A growing world of friendship, imagination, futuristic mystery and adventure.

UMIIMU is an original animated universe inspired by friendship, imagination, futuristic worlds, games, adventure, mystery, and everyday life experiences.

The universe follows UMI, IMU, and DUMIIMU as they discover strange dimensions, hidden portals, magical technology, connected worlds, and exciting adventures across different realities.

Alongside fantasy adventures and sci-fi storytelling, the UMIIMU universe will also feature heartwarming stories inspired by real-life moments, family experiences, friendships, day-to-day activities, celebrations, picnics, holidays, school life, gaming, creativity, and emotional journeys that people can relate to.

Created with a blend of stylized animation, playful storytelling, sci-fi fantasy, and collectible-inspired designs, UMIIMU is a growing entertainment universe filled with characters, stories, animations, games, artwork, digital experiences, collectibles, toys, accessories, apparel, merchandise, and future creative projects.
